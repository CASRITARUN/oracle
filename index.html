Bootstrap success patterns

Uses the directional model’s unseen holdout candles to learn whether a volatility-scaled target was reached before an adverse stop. Structural inputs include 20/50-bar range position and breakouts, session VWAP distance, opening-range position, gap, volume acceleration, compression/expansion, momentum acceleration, day position and higher-high/lower-low structure. 04 / PAPER OPTIONS

Calibrate real trade conversion

Completed paper option trades add spread, depth, delta, OI, volume, DTE, slippage and cost evidence that historical stock candles cannot provide.

Historical coverage

Actual stored data · no synthetic history

On the first successful Kite connection, Stock AI automatically starts a resumable one-year 5-minute backfill for the current F&O universe. The Stock AI universe is restricted to NFO option underlyings that also exist as NSE cash equities, so derivative indices are excluded. If Kite has no older candles for a newer/current equity instrument, that stock is marked MAX AVAILABLE and treated as resolved rather than retried forever. Heavy backfill automatically pauses whenever Stock AI is armed, has a live position, or has an unresolved live order; live execution and risk supervision always get priority. Directional training uses up to 20,000 stored candles per stock; the final 20% by time is reserved for validation. Historical setup validation remains underlying-candle evidence, not a historical options-profit backtest. Structural-pattern calculations reuse those stored candles and therefore do not add a per-stock API request.

Blended success selector

Historical prior + live option calibration

Historical setup probability is a conservative prior learned only from underlying candle paths. Live option-success probability comes from completed paper trades. When either validated success estimate is available, a candidate must clear both the configured minimum success probability and minimum expected-R threshold before it can be READY. While both success models are still learning, Stock AI uses the stricter fallback direction-confidence gate. Neither probability is a guaranteed win rate.

How this stockdesk operates

Paper first. The live gate requires a trained model and 60 closed paper trades spanning at least ten calendar days, a 42% net win rate, 0.05R net expectancy and a 1.2 profit factor. This is a time span, not ten distinct trading days. Explicit override bypasses paper-evidence eligibility only; it does not bypass the direction-model validation, option-liquidity, success-probability, expected-value or risk gates. Fresh entries do not require a second confirming candle; the latest completed bar is revalidated immediately before order submission.

Confirmed fills. Live orders use LIMIT orders with DAY validity; pending orders older than 45 seconds are submitted for cancellation during reconciliation. Cancellation is not immediate or guaranteed. Only broker-confirmed filled quantities change positions. Uncertain submissions require reconciliation.

Continuous supervision. Stops, partial profit-taking, targets, time exits and reversal checks run in the backend. A reversal exit requires a stable confirmed reversal, reducing one-candle whipsaws. There is no pre-square-off entry runway: qualified entries remain eligible until the configured square-off condition itself becomes active. A profitable trim moves the remaining stop to entry. Keep the process and connection running; these are not exchange-hosted protective stops.

Honest learning. Direction predicts stock movement, not profit certainty. The online model joins at 20% weight only after its own forward validation passes the minimum AUC.
